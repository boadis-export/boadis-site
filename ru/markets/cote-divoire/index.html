Cote d'Ivoire

Left-hand drive · used cars hard-capped at 5 years · TSV by engine size

Правила импорта

  • Used passenger cars must be manufactured within the last 5 years — no over-age penalty route.
  • Duty 5–20% under the UEMOA common tariff, plus 18% VAT and TSV of 0/10/20/35% by engine size.
  • COTECNA pre-shipment inspection (PVoC) is mandatory and must be done at the port of loading.
  • Port of Abidjan. Payment capacity C.

Источник: публикации таможни и органов по стандартизации, проверено 2026-11. Правила меняются — уточняйте перед отгрузкой.
