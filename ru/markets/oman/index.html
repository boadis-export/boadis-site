Oman

Left-hand drive · GCC spec · used max 5 years from first registration

Правила импорта

  • Customs duty 5% of CIF plus VAT 5% on the duty-paid value — about 10.25% of CIF in total.
  • Strict 5-year cap from the first registration date: in 2026 only cars first registered in 2021 or later qualify.
  • Fahas technical inspection is mandatory before the Royal Oman Police issues the Mulkiya.
  • Ports: Sohar and Salalah only — Port Sultan Qaboos in Muscat no longer handles vehicle imports.
