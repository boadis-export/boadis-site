Georgia

Left-hand drive accepted · no age cap · per-cc excise with a 6-year cliff

Правила импорта

  • Import duty is nominal: GEL 0.05 per cc plus 5% per year of age (a few dollars, not a percentage of CIF).
  • The real tax is excise: GEL 1.5/cc for cars 6 years or newer, GEL 4.5/cc for older (3× cliff, Apr 2026). EVs pay no excise.
  • VAT 18% on CIF + duty + excise. Euro 5 required, which in practice means built 2013 or later; no fixed age cap.
  • Port: Poti (Black Sea RoRo). Georgia is outside the EAEU, so no Russian recycling fee.

Источник: публикации таможни и органов по стандартизации, проверено 2026-11. Правила меняются — уточняйте перед отгрузкой.
