Нигерия

Left-hand drive · max 12 years · VehCAP pre-shipment inspection

Правила импорта

  • Passenger cars up to 12 years old; trucks up to 10 years.
  • Euro 4 emission standard or better.
  • VehCAP pre-shipment certification mandatory since 1 Aug 2026 — no certificate, no clearance.
  • Import duty reduced since 1 Jul 2026; expect 42–45% total tax burden including levies.
  • Left-hand drive only — right-hand drive vehicles cannot be registered.

Источник: публикации таможни и органов по стандартизации, проверено 2026-11. Правила меняются — уточняйте перед отгрузкой.
