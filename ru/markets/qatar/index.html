Qatar

Left-hand drive · used up to 5 years · 5% duty, no VAT

Правила импорта

  • Duty 5% of CIF under the GCC tariff; VAT has not been implemented yet (0%).
  • Used private imports are capped at about 5 years from manufacture, enforced at registration.
  • Gulf spec and left-hand drive; right-hand drive cannot be registered.
  • Port: Hamad Port (Umm Al Houl), 18–30 days from Ningbo. Payment capacity A.

Источник: публикации таможни и органов по стандартизации, проверено 2026-11. Правила меняются — уточняйте перед отгрузкой.
