Panama

Left-hand drive · used max 10 years · duty rises with vehicle age

Правила импорта

  • Import duty is ad valorem and age-banded: roughly 5–15% for new cars and progressively higher for used ones.
  • VAT 7% on the duty-paid value — one of the lowest in the region.
  • Used vehicles up to 10 years old; a technical inspection before registration is required.
  • Colón Free Zone handles transhipment; Balboa and Cristóbal are the main RoRo berths on the Pacific side.

Источник: публикации таможни и органов по стандартизации, проверено 2026-11. Правила меняются — уточняйте перед отгрузкой.

Логистика

Порт: Balboa · Cristóbal (Colón)
Транзит: 30–38 days from Ningbo
Способ: RoRo or 40HQ container — ask for the next sailing
На что обратить внимание: RoRo space 4–6 weeks ahead; EV units need UN38.3 + MSDS

The duty band depends on the model year at import, so a 3-year-old unit can land materially cheaper than a 7-year-old one.
