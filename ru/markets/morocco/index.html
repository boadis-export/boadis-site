Morocco

Left-hand drive · used under 5 years · duty 17.5% unless EU origin

Правила импорта

  • Passenger cars must be under 5 years old at the customs declaration date.
  • Duty 17.5% on the ADII assessed value, plus 20% VAT and 0.25% parafiscal tax — about 41% of CIF.
  • The 0% duty rate requires a valid EUR.1 EU origin certificate; Chinese-origin cars do not qualify.
  • Ports: Casablanca, Tanger Med. Payment capacity B.

Источник: публикации таможни и органов по стандартизации, проверено 2026-11. Правила меняются — уточняйте перед отгрузкой.
