Accueil / Marchés / Kuwait

Kuwait

Left-hand drive · cars 5 years · personal import restricted

Règles d'importation

  • Duty 5% of CIF; VAT not implemented yet (0%); no excise on cars.
  • Passenger cars and light commercial up to 5 years; trucks up to 10 years.
  • Personal import is limited to Kuwaiti nationals and diplomats — everyone else needs a licensed importer.
  • Port: Shuwaikh, 20–32 days from Ningbo. Payment capacity A.

Source : publications des douanes et des organismes de normalisation, vérifiées en 2026-11. Les règles changent — confirmez avant expédition.

Taxes et coût rendu (2026)

Droit d'importationDuty 5% of CIF (GCC common tariff)
TVA et autres taxesVAT 0% (signed the GCC framework but the 2026–2030 fiscal plan excludes VAT)
Taxe à la consommation0% — proposed only, not legislated
Coût renduCIF × 1.05 + fixed charges; landed ≈1.17–1.28× FOB (fixed charges weigh more on cheap cars)
