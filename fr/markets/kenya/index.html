Kenya

Right-hand drive · max 8 years from first registration · KEBS pre-shipment

Règles d'importation

  • Right-hand drive only. Used cars up to 8 years from first registration (2026 imports: registered 2019 or later).
  • Duty 25% + excise 20–30% by engine size + VAT 16% + IDF 3.5% + RDL 2% — budget 75–90% of CIF.
  • Customs values on CRSP, not on your invoice; KEBS pre-shipment inspection is mandatory.
  • Payment capacity B. Mombasa port; Nairobi ICD for inland delivery.

Source : publications des douanes et des organismes de normalisation, vérifiées en 2026-11. Les règles changent — confirmez avant expédition.
