Algérie

Left-hand drive · under 3 years · diesel banned

Règles d'importation

  • Vehicles must be under 3 years old at import.
  • Diesel passenger cars are not admitted.
  • Left-hand drive only.
  • Strong 2026 demand growth; payment capacity rated A
