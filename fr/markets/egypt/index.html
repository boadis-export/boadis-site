Accueil / Marchés / Egypt

Egypt

Left-hand drive · used max 3 years (build year) · EV pays 0% duty

Règles d'importation

  • Used passenger cars up to 3 years, counted from the model year of manufacture.
  • ICE and hybrid duty runs roughly 40–60% by engine size; pure EVs pay 0% duty and only 14% VAT.
  • Euro 4 minimum. CargoX e-documents mandatory since Jul 2021 — the B/L must be registered before arrival.
  • Ports: Port Said, Alexandria, Ain Sokhna. Payment capacity B.

Source : publications des douanes et des organismes de normalisation, vérifiées en 2026-11. Les règles changent — confirmez avant expédition.

Taxes et coût rendu (2026)

Droit d'importationDuty about 40–60% of CIF for passenger cars (banded by displacement under the "Arab and foreign vehicles" schedule); BEV 0%
TVA et autres taxesVAT 14% (base: CIF + duty + surcharges); a BEV pays only this 14%
Taxe à la consommationNo separate excise; a development surcharge applies at the current rate
Coût renduPetrol: CIF × (1 + 40–60%) × 1.14 + surcharge; BEV: CIF × 1.14
Limite d'âgeUsed passenger cars 3 years max, counted from build year; no published cap for BEV
Sourcehttps://ezautoglobal.com/zh/market-egypt+ https://www.customs.gov.eg/
