Türkiye

Left-hand drive · used-car import banned · ÖTV on top of duty

Import rules

  • New cars only — Türkiye does not allow import of used passenger cars.
  • Duty 10% for non-EU origin, then ÖTV (special consumption tax) at 45–220% depending on engine size and power, then 20% VAT on the whole chain. ÖTV dominates the price.
  • Conformity to Turkish type approval (or EU approval) is required before registration; a Chinese domestic unit does not qualify as-is.
  • Most Chinese brands now enter through local distribution or local assembly — check whether your model is already represented before competing on price.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
