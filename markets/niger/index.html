Niger

Left-hand drive · landlocked · Cotonou or Lomé corridor

Import rules

  • Duty around 20% plus 19% VAT; the unit is cleared in Cotonou, Lomé or Tema and then trucked inland.
  • No type approval that matters in practice; the transit bond and the inland carrier decide the timeline.
  • Used cars dominate — buyers care about parts availability and ground clearance more than badges.
  • Inland transport from Cotonou to Niamey is 1,000 km+ and adds both cost and damage risk.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
