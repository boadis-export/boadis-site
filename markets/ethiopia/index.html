Ethiopia

Left-hand drive · electric-only imports · Djibouti corridor

Import rules

  • Ethiopia stopped issuing import permits for petrol and diesel cars — plan electric only unless you hold a pre-existing licence.
  • Electric vehicles carry a low duty; 15% VAT applies on CIF plus duty.
  • No local homologation body for passenger cars in practice; customs checks documents and VIN.
  • All cargo lands at Djibouti and moves 800 km inland by road or rail — allow 40–50 days door to door.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
