Chile

Left-hand drive · 3CV homologation · no age limit

Import rules

  • 6% import duty plus 19% VAT — one of the lowest tax burdens in Latin America.
  • New cars need 3CV homologation by an authorised laboratory; used units need an emissions certificate before registration.
  • No age limit, but inspection is strict: rust, tyres and emissions are all checked.
  • Payment capacity A. 2026 imports ≈ 99,500 units — the deepest passenger-car market in South America.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
