Egypt

Left-hand drive · used max 3 years (build year) · EV pays 0% duty

Import rules

  • Used passenger cars up to 3 years, counted from the model year of manufacture.
  • ICE and hybrid duty runs roughly 40–60% by engine size; pure EVs pay 0% duty and only 14% VAT.
  • Euro 4 minimum. CargoX e-documents mandatory since Jul 2021 — the B/L must be registered before arrival.
  • Ports: Port Said, Alexandria, Ain Sokhna. Payment capacity B.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.

Taxes & landed cost (2026)

Import dutyDuty about 40–60% of CIF for passenger cars (banded by displacement under the "Arab and foreign vehicles" schedule); BEV 0%
VAT & other chargesVAT 14% (base: CIF + duty + surcharges); a BEV pays only this 14%
Special consumption taxNo separate excise; a development surcharge applies at the current rate
Landed costPetrol: CIF × (1 + 40–60%) × 1.14 + surcharge; BEV: CIF × 1.14
Age limitUsed passenger cars 3 years max, counted from build year; no published cap for BEV
Sourcehttps://ezautoglobal.com/zh/market-egypt+ https://www.customs.gov.eg/
