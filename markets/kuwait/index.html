Kuwait

Left-hand drive · cars 5 years · personal import restricted

Import rules

  • Duty 5% of CIF; VAT not implemented yet (0%); no excise on cars.
  • Passenger cars and light commercial up to 5 years; trucks up to 10 years.
  • Personal import is limited to Kuwaiti nationals and diplomats — everyone else needs a licensed importer.
  • Port: Shuwaikh, 20–32 days from Ningbo. Payment capacity A.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
