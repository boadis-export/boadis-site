Paraguay

Left-hand drive and RHD both accepted · used cars ≤10 years · 2026 YTD 26,000+ units

Import rules

  • DAI 6% + ISC 25% (rises with vehicle age, roughly 16% to 50%+) + IVA 10% + 0.5% service fee
  • Landed cost ≈1.35–1.37× CIF on a newer car, ≈1.45–1.50× on a 7–10 year old
  • Age limit: about 10 years (older cars become uneconomic because ISC scales with age)
  • Landlocked: ship to Iquique (Chile) then truck to Asunción / Ciudad del Este

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
