Jordan

Left-hand drive · max 5 years · special tax by fuel type

Import rules

  • Customs duty is 0%, but a special tax applies: 55–81% for petrol by engine size, 45% hybrid, 40% plug-in hybrid, 25% EV.
  • 16% general sales tax is charged on CIF plus the special tax.
  • Used cars must be under 5 years old (7 for EVs); left-hand drive only.
  • Aqaba free zone is the usual entry point — units can be re-exported to Iraq and Syria from there.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
