Ghana

Left-hand drive · max 10 years for used · Tema / Takoradi

Import rules

  • Duty 5–35% by engine size and value, plus 15% VAT, 2.5% NHIL, 2.5% GETFund and 1% COVID levy — budget about 21% on top of CIF.
  • Used vehicles must be under 10 years old; over-age units are refused entry at Tema.
  • Ghana Standards Authority inspects at the port; a clean inspection report from origin speeds clearance.
  • Tema handles most roll-on/roll-off; Takoradi is slower but less congested.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
