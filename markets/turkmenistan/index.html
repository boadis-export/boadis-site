Turkmenistan

Landlocked · transit via Kazakhstan or Uzbekistan · left-hand drive

Import rules

  • Vehicles clear through the State Customs Service; duty and excise are revised by decree and are quoted case by case — confirm the current rate with a local broker before you fix a price.
  • Left-hand drive only. Age limits are applied per shipment, and older units attract a higher excise.
  • Volume runs through a small number of state-registered importers, and settlement is often routed through a third-country account — a local partner is effectively required.
  • No new-energy incentives yet and charging is thin outside Ashgabat, so petrol models carry the volume.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
