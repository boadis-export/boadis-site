Nepal

Left-hand drive · used effectively closed · duty progressive by engine size

Import rules

  • Duty is progressive by engine size and can reach 80% for large-capacity cars.
  • VAT 13% on the duty-paid value.
  • Used imports are effectively closed; the market runs on new vehicles.
  • Landlocked: cargo discharges at Kolkata or Haldia and moves by rail/road to Birgunj.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
