Syria

Left-hand drive · sanctions-sensitive banking · Latakia / Tartus

Import rules

  • Customs duty on passenger cars is high and assessed per engine size; consumption tax is added on top.
  • Sanctions mean most international banks will not process Syrian-related payments — settle through a third-country entity and check every counterparty.
  • No usable type approval; registration is handled locally by the importer's agent.
  • Units enter through Latakia or Tartus; some cargo is transhipped via Beirut or Mersin.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
