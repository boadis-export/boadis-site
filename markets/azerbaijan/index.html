Azerbaijan

Left-hand drive · excise by engine size · Baku via Poti / Kulevi

Import rules

  • Import duty plus an excise that steps up with engine size; 18% VAT on CIF plus duty and excise.
  • Right-hand drive is banned; units must meet Euro-4 or newer to be registered.
  • Most cargo is railed or trucked from Georgian ports (Poti, Kulevi) or via Russia — check the current route before quoting.
  • Payment in hard currency is workable; confirm the buyer's bank before shipment.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
