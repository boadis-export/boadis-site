Colombia

Left-hand drive · new vehicles only · Euro 4 minimum

Import rules

  • Duty 35% of CIF, plus 19% VAT and an 8–16% consumption tax by value band.
  • Used vehicles are effectively banned — only 30+ year classics and diplomatic cases.
  • New vehicles must be 0–2 years from model year and comply with Resolution 5198 / Euro 4.
  • Ports: Cartagena, Buenaventura. Payment capacity B.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
