Iraq

Left-hand drive · no age cap · tariff revised Nov 2026

Import rules

  • The vehicle customs tariff was raised in November 2026 — confirm the rate with the clearing agent before you quote.
  • No age limit for passenger cars; registration practice differs between Baghdad, Basra and Erbil.
  • Left-hand drive only. Most units arrive through the UAE or Jordan re-export.
  • Payment capacity C: cash or confirmed L/C, and allow extra days for port clearance.
