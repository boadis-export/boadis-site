Armenia

Left-hand drive only · EAEU clearance · EV quota in 2026

Import rules

  • EAEU common tariff: about 15% of CIF for company imports; individuals pay an age- and engine-size EUR/cc unified payment instead.
  • VAT 20% on CIF + duty. Right-hand drive cannot be registered in Armenia.
  • 2026 EV window: an EAEU quota of 15,000 units duty-free plus VAT exemption until 31 Dec 2026 (units built 2024–2026).
  • No sea port — cars arrive at Poti (Georgia) and move overland via Bagratashen/Sadakhlo, 35–45 days.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
