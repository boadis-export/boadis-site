United Arab Emirates

Left-hand drive · no age cap · 5% duty + 5% VAT

Import rules

  • No general age limit for registration (RTA), but right-hand drive cannot be registered.
  • Duty 5% + VAT 5%. Re-export from Jebel Ali is VAT-free.
  • Vehicles must pass MoIAT status check — fire, flood, salvage and dismantled cars refused.
  • Payment capacity A. 125,815 units in 2026 — a re-export hub, not end demand.

Source: customs / standards authority publications, verified 2026-11. Rules change — confirm before shipment.
