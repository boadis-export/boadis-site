Services

From inquiry to delivery — what we handle and what you handle.

1. Inquiry & specification

Model, quantity, destination port, configuration.

2. Quotation

FOB price, validity, payment terms, certification responsibility.

3. Order & deposit

PI, deposit, production or stock allocation.

4. Inspection & photos

Pre-shipment check, photos and video before loading.

5. Export licence & customs

Licence application, declaration, commodity inspection.

6. Shipping

Container loading, seal, B/L, insurance.

7. Delivery & after-sales

Documents handover, destination clearance support, warranty.

Head-unit language localisation

After the car leaves the line we flash the infotainment menus and the voice prompts to the buyer's language. English is standard; Russian, Arabic, Spanish and French can be added per order.

  • Menu text and voice prompts, not just the UI language list
  • Factory software and warranty untouched — only the language pack changes
  • A short video of the finished unit goes to you before loading
  • Done per unit, so mixed-language orders are fine

Process map — who does what

The same flow we run internally, written out. Nothing here is outsourced to a third party without telling you.

# Step Owner
1Confirm model, quantity and colour; quote from the current price listSales · Procurement & Finance
2Sales signs the PI with you; Procurement signs the supply contract in parallelSales · Procurement & Finance
3You pay the deposit per PI; we pay our supplier per contractSales · Finance
4VIN and factory photos are collected as the unit comes off the line, then sent to youDocumentation · Sales
5Balance against VIN and photosSales
6Licence path splits: new cars go through the licensed exporter; used cars are bought on an internal trade contract and applied for on our own channelProcurement · Documentation
7Export licence issued, then booking and customs declarationDocumentation · Sales
8Delivery, document handover, after-salesSales · Service

Two licence routes: new cars move under a licensed exporter's quota — we supply the documents and pay for the unit; used cars are bought by us on an internal trade contract, which lets us apply for the export licence on our own channel.

Documents provided

Commercial invoice · Packing list · Sales contract · Customs declaration · Bill of lading · Certificate of origin · Certification (as applicable) · Insurance policy

Lead time: 30–45 days after deposit for stock models, 60–75 days for factory orders. Warranty and claim process are stated in the sales contract.
