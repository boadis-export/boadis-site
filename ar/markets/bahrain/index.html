البحرين

Left-hand drive · GCC spec · no age limit

قواعد الاستيراد

  • Customs duty 5% of CIF under the GCC common tariff; VAT 10% on the duty-paid value — the highest VAT in the Gulf.
  • No fixed age limit on used imports: only Bahrain and the UAE have none among the six GCC states.
  • Tasdeeq technical inspection is mandatory; VIN and chassis must match the paperwork exactly.
  • Port: Khalifa Bin Salman (Hidd), 22–34 days from Busan via Jebel Ali or Salalah.
