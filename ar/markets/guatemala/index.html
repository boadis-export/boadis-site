غواتيمالا

Left-hand drive · used max 10 years · duty 10–20% by age band

قواعد الاستيراد

  • Duty runs 10–20% ad valorem, banded by vehicle age — older units pay the higher rate.
  • VAT 12% on the duty-paid value; first registration also carries a municipal circulation fee.
  • Used cars up to 10 years old; a customs inspection at Puerto Quetzal or Santo Tomás is mandatory.
  • Both coasts work: Puerto Quetzal (Pacific) for the capital, Santo Tomás (Caribbean) for the east.
