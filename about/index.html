About us

BOADIS EXPORT & IMPORT CO., LIMITED · 香港博远进出口有限公司

Incorporated in Hong Kong on 30 April 2026 · Company No. 80307002 · Business Registration No. 80307002-000-04-26-5

BOADIS EXPORT & IMPORT CO., LIMITED is a Hong Kong based vehicle exporter working with Geely, Lynk & Co, Zeekr, Jetour, Changan and Wuling. We supply new vehicles and Geely's official circular-car units, shipping from Ningbo and Nansha to Africa, the Middle East, Latin America and Central Asia. Our work covers model selection against destination duty and homologation rules, export licensing, inspection, loading and documents — one invoice, one point of contact.
